# Code of Conduct

Pricelight adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and accept
constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people
this project serves: patients trying to understand what care will cost.
Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/lobojoel2003/Pricelight/security/advisories/new)
(it is private to maintainers) or by contacting a maintainer directly.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits
or contributions, or ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines
of the Contributor Covenant.
